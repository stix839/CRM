Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short version: this website has no forms, no ads and no tracking. We only get your information if you call, text or email us, and we never sell it.

Who we are

M2 Media Management ("M2", "we", "us") is a social media marketing team. This policy covers this website, m2-mediamanagement.com, and the information you share with us when you contact us or become a client.

Information this website collects

This website doesn't ask you for any personal information. It has no sign-up forms, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking cookies.

Like almost every website, the companies that host and deliver it automatically handle some technical information so the page can load and stay protected from abuse. This includes your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time of the request. Our hosting provider (Railway) and our network and security provider (Cloudflare) process this information. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell real visitors from automated traffic.

The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google describes how it handles that information in its privacy policy.

Information you give us

If you call, text or email us, or download one of our contact cards and reach out, we receive what you choose to share. That's usually your name, phone number, email address and details about your business. We use it to:

  • reply to you and schedule a call or meeting
  • prepare a proposal for your business
  • provide and support our services if you become a client
  • keep records of our conversations with you

We keep this information in our own customer records and email and phone accounts. We don't sell it, rent it or share it with anyone for their marketing.

If you're a client

To run your social media, we may need access to your Facebook and Instagram pages and to photos and videos of your business. We use that access and content only to provide the services you asked for. You can remove our access to your accounts at any time, and we stop using your content for new posts when our work together ends. We may show work we made for you as an example of our services unless you ask us not to.

Who we share information with

We only share information with service providers that help us run our business, such as our email, phone, hosting and customer records providers, and only so they can do that work for us. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and client information for as long as we're talking or working together, and afterwards only as long as we need it for our records or as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email or call us using the details below and we'll take care of it. If you don't want to hear from us again, just tell us.

Children

This website and our services are for businesses. They aren't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
