Questions

Social media marketing, answered.

What business owners ask us most before we start growing their page.

What does M2 Media Management do?

We're a social media marketing team for business owners. We create your photos and videos, run your Facebook and Instagram on a steady schedule, and turn the people who see your work into messages, calls and new customers.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business that does great work but doesn't have the time to post about it. Contractors, home service companies, shops and other local businesses all win more customers when their page is active and professional.

Do I have to pay for ads?

No. We grow your own Facebook and Instagram pages with real content, so you're not paying for ads or for leads that get sold to your competitors too.

Do I have to make the content myself?

No. We capture your work, your team and your results with photos and video, and we write every post in your voice. You stay focused on running your business.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

We focus on Facebook and Instagram. We keep both pages active, consistent and professional, and make it easy for the people who find you there to reach you.

How much does social media management cost?

It depends on your business and how much content you need. Book a 20-minute call and we'll show you what we'd post for you and exactly what it would cost.

How do I get started?
